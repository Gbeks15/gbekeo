## The problem

Small service businesses run on people doing everything by hand.
Answering enquiries. Juggling bookings. Posting content. Chasing the numbers at the end of the month.

None of it is hard. All of it takes time away from the actual work.

## What I built

Elon is an agentic assistant that handles the back office for two UK service businesses.

- handles customer enquiries
- coordinates scheduling
- picks and posts Instagram content every day, straight from Google Drive
- runs finance workflows, including VAT calculations and accounts
- sends reports so nobody has to build them

I talk to it through Telegram, like I would a member of staff.

## How it's built

- Python and n8n for the workflows
- Claude and ChatGPT APIs with tool and function calling
- Telegram, Instagram and Google Drive integrations
- Running on a VPS

## My role

Built end to end, on zero budget. It's live and the businesses use it every day.
